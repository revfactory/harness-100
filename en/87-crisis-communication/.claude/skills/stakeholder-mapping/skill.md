# Stakeholder Mapping — Crisis Stakeholder Mapping

Enhances the stakeholder analysis capabilities of the situation-analyst and message-strategist agents.

## Crisis Grade Classification System

| Grade | Criteria | Response Time | Decision Maker |
|-------|----------|--------------|----------------|
| Level 1 (Minor) | Internal impact, low media interest | 24 hours | Department Head |
| Level 2 (Moderate) | Customer impact, regional media | 12 hours | Division Head / Executive |
| Level 3 (Serious) | Widespread impact, national media | 4 hours | C-Level |
| Level 4 (Crisis) | Threat to life/safety or organizational survival | 1 hour | CEO + Board |

### Stakeholder Classification During a Crisis

| Stakeholder | Key Concern | Core Message | Channel | Frequency |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| Victims / Families | Safety, compensation, sincerity | Apology, support commitment | Direct in-person | Immediate + ongoing |
| Employees | Job security, safety, information | Facts, roles, action guidance | Internal channels | Immediate |
| Customers | Service continuity, trust | Scope of impact, response measures | Official channels | Within 4 hours |
| Media | Facts, accountability, response | Official position, timeline | Press release / press conference | Regular cadence |
| Regulators | Legal compliance, corrective action | Reporting, cooperative intent | Official reports | By statutory deadline |
| Investors | Financial impact, governance | Impact assessment, response plan | IR disclosure | Within 24 hours |
| Community | Safety, environmental impact | Scope, recovery plan | Local media, briefings | Within 48 hours |

## Crisis Communication Timeline

### Golden Hour (First 60 Minutes)

```
0–15 min: Verify facts, determine crisis grade
15–30 min: Convene crisis response team, collect initial information
30–45 min: Draft core messages, issue internal notification
45–60 min: Issue external initial statement (minimum "aware + investigating")
```

### 24-Hour Response Roadmap

| Time | Activity | Deliverable |
|------|----------|------------|
| 0–1h | Initial response | Internal notification, initial statement |
| 1–4h | Fact-finding | Situation report v1 |
| 4–8h | Official response | Press release, Q&A |
| 8–12h | Stakeholder communication | Customer notice, employee message |
| 12–24h | Response measures announcement | Improvement plan, follow-up press release |

## Message Framing Strategy

### Tone by Crisis Type

| Crisis Type | Responsibility Level | Tone | Key Elements |
|-------------|---------------------|------|-------------|
| Victim type (natural disaster, etc.) | Low | Compassion, resolve | Support, recovery, resilience |
| Accident type (no intent) | Moderate | Regret, accountability | Facts, actions, prevention |
| Negligence type (unintentional fault) | High | Apology, ownership | Acknowledgment, compensation, improvement |
| Intentional type (concealment, violation) | Very high | Sincere apology | Full admission, structural reform |

| Item | Criterion |
|------|-----------|
